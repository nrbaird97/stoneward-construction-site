How they are built

Built to the same code as a site-built home.

These are not mobile homes. These plans are designed to the International Residential Code, the same code your city applies to a house built on site, and they are set on a permanent foundation.

Residential code, not HUD

The factory builds to IRC and IBC requirements rather than the HUD code used for manufactured homes. State approvals for the modules are handled by the factory.

Framed and insulated like a house

The standard wall section shows 2x6 exterior studs with R-19 insulation, R-38 in the roof and R-30 in the floor. Your plan set confirms the details for your home.

Inspected along the way

Inspections are documented with third-party inspectors during the build, and you are invited to walk the home before drywall and again before it ships.

A real foundation

Plans can be set on a crawl space, a basement, a slab or piers, depending on your lot and what your jurisdiction requires. We help you sort out which one fits.
