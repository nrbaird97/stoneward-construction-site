About Lehi

Tech corridor, old Main Street, and a band of in-between lots.

Lehi anchors the north end of Utah Valley. The I-15 corridor that people call Silicon Slopes runs through it, Thanksgiving Point is a regional draw, and the historic downtown sits well apart from the newer hillside and lakeside subdivisions. Between the older neighborhoods and the new ones are plenty of quarter-acre lots.

That is where Lehi's change lands. A lot of 11,000 to 14,519 square feet could not host a detached ADU last spring, and it can now. The city's development director estimated eligible lots could grow by 20 percent or more. With only 6 feet required between the unit and the main house, narrow, long plans are the natural fit.

What this means for a Lehi homeowner.

Lots between a quarter and a third of an acre that were out before can qualify now.

Close to where people work

A parent suite or a long-term rental near the tech corridor is exactly the case this change was written for.

Routing utilities on a mid-size Lehi lot.

On a lot just over 11,000 square feet the cost driver is usually how far lines have to run around the house to reach the new unit. If sewer and water are at the back corner of the lot, a unit placed there is cheap to serve, and the same unit at the opposite side can cost much more. We walk the lot and price the trenching, tie-ins and foundation before you decide where the unit goes.

Questions

Lehi ADU questions, answered.

What changed in Lehi in 2026?

Lehi already allowed detached ADUs, but only on lots of 14,520 square feet or more. In July 2026 the city lowered that to the state's 11,000 square feet and required detached units to be at least six feet from the main house, according to Deseret News.

How many more Lehi lots qualify now?

Lehi's development director estimated that eligible lots could grow by 20 percent or more. Your own lot depends on its exact square footage, so check the parcel size before you plan.

Can I have an ADU inside the house and a detached one?

No. Lehi's guidance allows only one ADU per lot, so you cannot approve one in the main home and another in a detached building.

How much parking does a Lehi ADU need?

State law caps the requirement at one space under 650 square feet and two at 650 square feet or more. Lehi's guidance asks for two spaces for a detached unit over 650 square feet.

Where do I apply?

Lehi Planning handles the detached ADU application. We help you assemble the site plan, floor plan and utility information it asks for.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with Lehi before you design or buy.
