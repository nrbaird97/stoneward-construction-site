Centerville, Davis County

Centerville cut its detached ADU lot minimum to 8,500 square feet.

Centerville's City Council approved Ordinance 2026-20 this fall, adding a new Chapter 12.58 for detached ADUs. The council set the minimum lot at 8,500 square feet and the height at 16 feet, which reaches many more lots than the state's 11,000 square foot rule but keeps units low.

Adopted Sep 2026

Ordinance
Ordinance 2026-20, new Chapter 12.58 (DADU Standards) and an amended table of uses in section 12.36.020
Lot
At least 8,500 sq ft, reduced by the Council
Height
16 ft maximum, measured with the city method based on the average height from eave to peak
Location
Not in the front yard of the main home. On corner lots, the secondary front yard, the narrow side yard behind the home and the rear yard are eligible

Utilities

About Centerville

A foothill city that wrote a low-profile rule.

Centerville sits between Bountiful and Farmington along the Davis County foothills, with Lagoon on its western side and neighborhoods that climb toward the mountains. Many of its streets have mature trees, established landscaping and a settled feel, and part of the city is the Deuel Creek Historic District.

The council's 16 foot height cap is the detail that stands out. It uses the city's average eave-to-peak method, so a roof pitch that works elsewhere may not here, and a two-story plan is unlikely to fit. Staff also told the Planning Commission they expect a revised version within months once real applications show what needs fixing, so confirm current text before you finalize a design.

What it means for you

What this means for a Centerville homeowner.

8,500 square foot minimum

Lower than the state 11,000, so more lots qualify. Measure yours.

16 foot height, eave to peak average

The city measures height as an average between eave and peak, so roof pitch and plate height matter more than overall box size.

A detached and an internal ADU

One lot can have both, as long as parking and lot coverage are met for each.

Historic district overlay

Rules for the Deuel Creek Historic District and the new DADU chapter interact in ways the city still has to sort out.

Hillside lots, corner lots and a rear-yard rule.

Because the unit cannot sit in the front yard, the rear yard or a corner lot's secondary front yard is where it goes, and on a sloped foothill lot that may mean retaining walls or extra grading. We survey the pad, check the utility run and look at crane access before you choose a plan.

Questions

Centerville ADU questions, answered.

Has Centerville approved detached ADUs?

Yes. Planning Commission minutes of September 23, 2026 record a debrief of the City Council deliberations and approval of Ordinance 2026-20 governing detached accessory dwelling units.

What lot size does Centerville require?

The Council reduced the minimum lot size to 8,500 square feet, according to those minutes. State law requires cities to allow DADUs on lots of 11,000 square feet or more but lets them go lower.

How tall can a Centerville ADU be?

16 feet, using the city method based on the average height from eave to peak.

Can I have a detached ADU and an internal ADU?

Yes. The Council approved allowing one lot to have both, as long as parking and lot coverage requirements are met for each.

Where on the lot can it go?

Not in the front yard of the main home. On a corner lot, it can go in the secondary front yard, the narrow side yard behind the home or the rear yard.

Do I need a separate utility connection?

The Council let property owners decide whether the unit has separate or shared utility connections.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with Centerville before you design or buy.
