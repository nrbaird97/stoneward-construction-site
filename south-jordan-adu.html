South Jordan, Salt Lake County

South Jordan allows detached ADUs on lots as small as 6,000 square feet.

South Jordan's ADU guidebook, updated in September 2026, says a detached ADU needs a lot of at least 6,000 square feet in a zone where single-family homes are allowed, and that city staff approve the ADU permit administratively. That is well below the 11,000 square feet in Utah's October 2026 law. A matching code update, Ordinance 2026-24, went to the Planning Commission on September 8, 2026 'to conform with recent changes in state law'. We have not confirmed that the council adopted it.

Lot
Minimum 6,000 sq ft, in a zone where single-family is permitted
Size
No more than 50 percent of the main home living space or 1,500 sq ft, whichever is less
Height
No more than 5 ft above the main building, up to 25 ft
Setbacks
Zone setbacks apply, never closer than 10 ft to a property line. Above 16 ft tall the setback grows 1 ft for each foot of height over 16 ft
Lot coverage
Zone maximum: 20 percent in A-5, 30 in A-1, 40 in R-1.8, R-2.5, R-3 and R-4, 50 in R-5, 60 in R-M
Parking
At least one off-street space that does not block the main home required parking
Occupancy
The owner lives in the main home or the detached ADU
Rentals
30 calendar days or more. No short-term rentals
Permit
An ADU permit, approved by staff, before a building permit. Needs a site plan, floor plan, elevations and an ADU affidavit recorded with Salt Lake County

Not covered by the guide. ADUs there are only permitted above a detached or semi-detached garage

About South Jordan

Newer subdivisions, Daybreak and generous lot sizes.

South Jordan runs from older streets near the river bottom up to newer hillside and master-planned neighborhoods in the southwest valley, and much of it was built in the last few decades on regular lots with wide streets. Daybreak, the large master-planned community, follows its own standards, and the city's ADU guide leaves it out.

The city's approach is unusually permissive on lot size and unusually specific on design. A detached unit can sit on a 6,000 square foot lot, but it must match the main home, stay 10 feet from the property lines, and keep windows and doors on upper floors away from neighbors, so the unit design matters as much as the lot.

What it means for you

What this means for a South Jordan homeowner.

6,000 square foot lots

The city guide sets 6,000 square feet for a detached ADU, much lower than the state 11,000, so more lots may qualify.

Up to 1,500 square feet

The cap is the smaller of 1,500 square feet and 50 percent of the main home living space.

Setback grows with height

The unit stays at least 10 feet from lot lines, plus 1 foot for each foot of height above 16 feet, up to a 25 foot limit.

Privacy rules for windows

Upper-floor windows and doors must be 20 feet from the lot line, high-set, or turned away from the line, and decks near lot lines are prohibited.

Getting it built in South Jordan

From a South Jordan lot to move-in.

The full process
  1. Check the lot and zone. It must be at least 6,000 square feet in a zone that allows a single-family home. In Daybreak, ask the city first.
  2. Check agreements. Ask Planning whether a development agreement from before May 6, 2026 limits ADUs on your lot, and whether Ordinance 2026-24 was adopted.
  3. Choose a plan. Stay under 1,500 square feet and half your main home living space.
  4. ADU permit. Apply through the city online development portal with a site plan, floor plan, elevations and the recorded affidavit.
  5. Building permit, set and finish. After the ADU permit, building permit, foundation and utilities, crane day and final inspection.

Questions

South Jordan ADU questions, answered.

Does South Jordan allow detached ADUs?

Yes. The city ADU guidebook, updated in September 2026, lists detached ADU requirements for single-family zones, with an administrative ADU permit. Daybreak is covered separately.

What lot size does a detached ADU need in South Jordan?

At least 6,000 square feet, according to the guidebook. We have not confirmed whether Ordinance 2026-24 changes this.

How big can a detached ADU be?

No more than 50 percent of the main home living space or 1,500 square feet, whichever is less.

How tall can it be?

No more than 5 feet taller than the main building, up to 25 feet. Over 16 feet tall, the setback from the property line increases by 1 foot for each foot of extra height.

Do I have to live on the property?

Yes. The guide requires owner occupancy of the main home or the detached ADU, and rentals must be for 30 calendar days or more.

Does it need a permit?

Yes. Every detached ADU needs an ADU permit, approved administratively by city staff, before the building permit.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with South Jordan before you design or buy.
