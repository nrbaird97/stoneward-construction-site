Mapleton, Utah County

Mapleton: from half an acre toward a quarter acre.

Mapleton's earlier code required 21,780 square feet, a half acre, for a detached accessory apartment. The City Council voted this year to amend its ADU code to comply with state law, which caps the lot size a city can require at 11,000 square feet.

About Mapleton

A foothill city built on bigger lots.

Mapleton sits at the southeast edge of Utah Valley, under Maple Mountain, with Springville and Spanish Fork as neighbors. The city has a semi-rural character, with larger lots than most of its neighbors and a history of orchards and open ground. That is why its old half-acre rule excluded so many homes, and why the move toward the state's quarter-acre ceiling is a bigger change here than in a city of small lots.

Mapleton's approach is also different in structure. Rather than a single size for every lot, the city ties the largest allowed ADU to lot size, so a larger parcel can host a larger unit. The council also kept an exemption for the Harvest Park subdivision, where narrow lots and covenants were the concern.

Questions

Mapleton ADU questions, answered.

What did Mapleton's code require before?

The earlier code required a lot of at least 21,780 square feet, which is half an acre, for a detached accessory apartment.

What does SB 284 change in Mapleton?

State law says a covered city cannot require more than 11,000 square feet for a detached ADU on a lot with a single-family home. The council voted this year to amend the ADU code to comply. We could not confirm the exact number in the adopted text, so confirm it with the city.

Is the ADU size tied to my lot size?

Mapleton kept size limits that scale by lot size and removed a 40 percent limit, according to local coverage of the council vote. A larger lot may allow a larger unit.

What is the Harvest Park exemption?

The council kept an existing exemption for the Harvest Park subdivision, where narrow lots and covenants were cited as reasons. If you live there, ask the city how it applies to you.

Can I put an ADU on a lot that uses septic?

It depends on the lot and what the city and county health authorities allow. Tell us up front and we will explain the options and costs before you commit.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with Mapleton before you design or buy.
