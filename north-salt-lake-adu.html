North Salt Lake, Davis County

North Salt Lake has allowed detached ADUs since 2018.

North Salt Lake did not wait for SB 284. The City Council approved ADUs on single-family lots with Ordinance 2018-14, and the regulations, amended April 1, 2025, now sit in code section 10-19-17. Detached cottages, units over a garage and tiny homes are all named in the code.

History
Ordinance 2018-14 allowed ADUs on single-family lots. Regulations amended April 1, 2025, now in section 10-19-17
Where
Within or attached to the home, over an attached or detached garage, or in a detached cottage, guest house or tiny home on a single-family lot in any zone
Size
Detached units 300 sq ft minimum and 1,200 sq ft maximum, no more than two bedrooms. Larger only with a Planning Commission conditional use permit
Setbacks
Behind the home: the zone side yard and 10 ft rear, or 5 ft rear with no windows or doors facing that line. Beside the home: the same setbacks as the main home
Second story
Allowed in an accessory structure only if it meets the main home height and setback standards
Lot coverage
The permit form lists 35 percent for all buildings and 25 percent of the rear yard
Parking
1 added space, 2 for two or more bedrooms, not in tandem with the main home parking. Within a quarter mile of a bus rapid transit route, the main home requirement can drop by 1
Occupancy
The owner lives in the main home or the ADU, with up to 3 years of temporary absence allowed. One ADU per lot
Utilities
Separate meters are allowed in the owner name. A separate water connection carries a development and connection fee
Permit
A land use permit with no fee, plus a building permit for new construction

Not confirmed: The maximum height for a single-story detached ADU, which the ADU section does not state. The general accessory building rule caps height at one story and 20 feet, but whether that governs an ADU is for the city to confirm. We also have not seen a 2026 update tied to SB 284.

About North Salt Lake

A hillside city with a long ADU track record.

North Salt Lake sits at the south tip of Davis County, between Salt Lake City and Bountiful, with neighborhoods that climb the foothills above I-15 and older streets near the city center. Many hillside lots fall in the city's Sensitive Lands overlay, and the council updated its geologic hazard and buildable envelope rules in December 2025.

What sets North Salt Lake apart is time. ADUs have been legal here since 2018, the land use permit is free, and the code names tiny homes and units over a garage alongside detached cottages. The city also gives a parking break near bus rapid transit, a rare rule on the Wasatch Front.

What it means for you

What this means for a North Salt Lake homeowner.

Legal since 2018

North Salt Lake approved ADUs on single-family lots with Ordinance 2018-14, long before SB 284, so the permit counter has seen them.

300 to 1,200 square feet

Detached units must be at least 300 and no more than 1,200 square feet, with up to two bedrooms. Larger takes a conditional use permit.

A 5 foot rear setback option

Behind the home, the rear setback is 10 feet, or 5 feet if no windows or doors face the rear line.

Separate meters allowed

The code lets the ADU have its own water, gas and electric meters in the owner name, with a fee for a separate water connection.

Questions

North Salt Lake ADU questions, answered.

Does North Salt Lake allow detached ADUs?

Yes. The City Council approved ADUs on single-family lots with Ordinance 2018-14, and the regulations in code section 10-19-17 were amended April 1, 2025. Detached cottages, guest houses, units over a garage and tiny homes are allowed.

How big can a detached ADU be?

At least 300 and no more than 1,200 square feet, with no more than two bedrooms. The Planning Commission can approve a larger unit with a conditional use permit and added conditions.

Is there a minimum lot size?

The ADU section does not set one. A lot must meet the parking, setback and lot coverage rules, including the 25 percent rear yard coverage limit.

Do I have to live on the property?

Yes. The owner must live in the main home or the ADU. A temporary absence of up to three years for a job assignment, sabbatical or voluntary service is allowed.

How much parking is needed?

One added off-street space, or two for an ADU with two or more bedrooms, not in tandem with the main home parking. Within a quarter mile of a bus rapid transit route, the main home requirement can drop by one space.

Is there a permit fee?

The city says there is no fee for the ADU land use permit. New construction also needs a building permit, which carries its own fees.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with North Salt Lake before you design or buy.
