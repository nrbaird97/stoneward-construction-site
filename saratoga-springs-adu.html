Saratoga Springs, Utah

Saratoga Springs has opened the door to backyard ADUs.

The Saratoga Springs City Council adopted its detached ADU rules on September 15, 2026, to comply with SB 284. Owners of qualifying lots can now plan a separate small home in the backyard, with local standards the city says it will keep refining.

Questions

Saratoga Springs ADU questions.

Will Saratoga Springs charge impact fees on a detached ADU?

The city said it may impose impact fees on detached ADUs, though not on internal ones. Ask the city for the current fee schedule early, because it affects your budget.

Does SB 284 apply to Saratoga Springs?

Yes. Saratoga Springs is covered by the state mandate, which requires a covered city to allow a detached ADU on a lot of 11,000 square feet or more that has a single-family home, as a permitted use. Cities can still set size, height, setback, owner-occupancy and rental rules within the limits of the law. Read the SB 284 guide.

How much parking does a detached ADU need?

A city cannot require more than one on-site space for a detached ADU under 650 square feet, or more than two for a unit of 650 square feet or larger.

Can I rent it out?

The state allows a city to require rentals of at least 90 consecutive days and to require the owner to live on the property. Check how Saratoga Springs applies those rules to your lot.

What if my lot is smaller than 11,000 square feet?

The state mandate does not reach it, but a city may choose to allow smaller lots. Ask Saratoga Springs Planning what applies to your parcel.
