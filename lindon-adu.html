Lindon, Utah County

Lindon is lowering its detached ADU lot minimum.

Lindon already allowed detached ADUs, but city staff said its 12,000 square foot minimum had to come down to match SB 284's 11,000. An ordinance implementing the state law was before the City Council on September 21, 2026, and Deseret News lists Lindon among the cities that adopted compliant rules.

About Lindon

A small city between two bigger neighbors.

Lindon is a smaller Utah County city between Pleasant Grove to the north and Orem to the south, with Mount Timpanogos to the east and Utah Lake to the west. It has a quiet, residential feel, with the Lindon Heritage Trail and a lakeside marina, and its lots often run larger than those in the surrounding cities.

The 12,000 square foot minimum is why the state change matters here. A lot between 11,000 and 12,000 square feet could not host a detached unit before and now can. The planning commission also tidied how the rules handle owner occupancy and struck a separate-sewer requirement, so check the final text rather than the older rule.

What it means for you

What this means for a Lindon homeowner.

12,000 down to 11,000

The state ceiling is lower than Lindon's old minimum, so lots between 11,000 and 12,000 square feet are the newly qualifying group.

No five-year residency rule

The commission recommended defining owner occupancy without a five-year residency requirement, while still requiring one unit to be owner-occupied.

Separate sewer struck

Staff were directed to strike a separate-sewer requirement, which could reduce hookup costs. Confirm the final text.

Parking relief

State law caps parking at one space for a unit under 650 square feet, which makes the smaller plans easier to place.

Sewer and water: the cost that was on the table.

One reason the sewer requirement got debated in Lindon is that hooking a new unit to the sewer can be a major cost. If the final text lets you tie into the existing lateral where it is approved, that can save real money, and if not it can cost much more. Questions

Lindon ADU questions, answered.

What lot size did Lindon require before?

City staff and a Utah County guide say Lindon allowed detached ADUs on lots of at least 12,000 square feet. State law says a covered city cannot require more than 11,000.

Has Lindon adopted a new ordinance?

An ordinance implementing SB 284, number 2026-16-O, was before the City Council on September 21, 2026, and Deseret News lists Lindon among the cities that adopted compliant rules. The staff report left the final adoption date blank, so confirm the adopted text with the city.

Does Lindon require owner occupancy?

The Planning Commission recommended requiring that one of the units be owner-occupied, with a clarified definition and no five-year residency requirement. Confirm the final wording.

Is a separate sewer hookup required?

Staff were directed to strike a separate-sewer requirement during the commission process. Confirm what the final ordinance says.

How much parking does a Lindon ADU need?

State law caps the requirement at one space under 650 square feet and two at 650 square feet or more.
