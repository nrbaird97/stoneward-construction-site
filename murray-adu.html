Murray, Salt Lake County

Murray allows backyard ADUs on 10,000 square foot lots, one story only.

Murray City's ADU page says a detached ADU needs a lot of at least 10,000 square feet, a single story no taller than 20 feet, and a floor area of no more than 50 percent of the main home and no more than 1,000 square feet. The council loosened these standards on November 19, 2024 (3 to 0), and the planning manager told the council about 20 percent of Murray lots are big enough. We found no 2026 Murray action tied to Utah's October 2026 detached ADU law, so check the lot minimum before you plan.

Official text: Murray City ADU page Murray City Council minutes, November 19, 2024 Murray City Code (American Legal Publishing)

Lot
Minimum 10,000 sq ft for a detached ADU, per the city ADU page and the planning manager at the 11/19/2024 council hearing
Count
One ADU per lot, in zones that allow single-family homes
Height
One story, maximum 20 ft
Size
No more than 50 percent of the main home floor area and no more than 1,000 sq ft. The council raised the share from 40 to 50 percent in 2024
Setbacks
Not allowed in the front yard. 10 ft from the rear and side lines, 20 ft on the street side of a corner lot
Parking
One added off-street space, for three on the property
Occupancy
The owner lives in the main home or the ADU. An affidavit is recorded with the Salt Lake County Recorder before the application is accepted
Rentals
No short-term rentals. Rent for stays under 30 days is prohibited. A long-term rental needs a Murray business license
Builder
Owner-building of ADUs is not permitted. A licensed contractor and a building permit are required

Not confirmed: Whether Murray has changed its 10,000 square foot minimum for the October 1, 2026 state law, which uses 11,000 for covered cities. The city ADU page is an undated summary, and we could not open Chapter 17.78 itself. Confirm the current text with Murray Planning before you design.

About Murray

Mid-valley lots, mature trees and a one-story rule.

Murray sits in the middle of the Salt Lake Valley, a built-out city of older brick and rambler homes, mature street trees and some larger lots, with the Murray Park and Fashion Place areas drawing people for work and transit. Most lots were platted decades ago, and the planning manager told the council in 2024 that roughly 20 percent of Murray lots reach 10,000 square feet and not all of those are suitable.

That is why Murray's detached ADU rule is so lot specific. At the 2024 hearing a resident worried about the city's 'City of Trees' character, and the council approved the looser standards anyway. If your lot qualifies, the design limits are strict: one story, 20 feet, 10 foot setbacks, and no more than 1,000 square feet, which suits a compact factory-built home.

What it means for you

What this means for a Murray homeowner.

A 10,000 square foot lot

Only about one in five Murray lots reaches the size, so confirm your lot on the county parcel map before choosing a plan.

One story, 20 feet

Detached units are limited to a single story at 20 feet, which rules out loft and two-story designs.

1,000 square feet or half your house

The cap is the smaller of 1,000 square feet and 50 percent of the main home floor area.

Licensed contractor required

Murray does not allow owner-building of ADUs, so a licensed contractor pulls the building permit.

Plans that fit Murray

Single-story plans that fit a 1,000 square foot cap.

Getting it built in Murray

From a Murray lot to move-in.

The full process
  1. Check the lot. Confirm it is at least 10,000 square feet and in a zone that allows a single-family home.
  2. Confirm the current rule. Ask Murray Planning whether the lot minimum changed for the state law.
  3. Choose a plan. Single story, under 1,000 square feet and under half your main home floor area.
  4. Affidavit and permit. Record the occupancy and rental affidavit with Salt Lake County, then apply with the city ADU form and a building permit through a licensed contractor.
  5. Site work, set and finish. Foundation and utilities, crane day, then finish work and final inspection before anyone moves in.

Questions

Murray ADU questions, answered.

Does Murray allow detached ADUs?

Yes. The Murray City ADU page allows one ADU per lot in zones that allow single-family homes, and lists standards for detached units. The council approved changes to the detached standards on November 19, 2024.

What lot size does a detached ADU need in Murray?

At least 10,000 square feet, according to the city ADU page and the planning manager at the 2024 council hearing. We could not confirm whether this changed for the 2026 state law.

How tall and how big can a detached ADU be?

One story and no more than 20 feet. Floor area is limited to 50 percent of the main home and to 1,000 square feet.

Can I build it myself?

No. Murray says owner-building of ADUs is not permitted, so a licensed contractor must build it under a city building permit.

Can I rent it on a short-term basis?

No. Murray prohibits short-term rentals of ADUs and rent for stays under 30 days. A long-term rental needs a Murray business license.

How much parking does it need?

One added off-street space, for three total on the property, according to the city ADU page.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with Murray before you design or buy.
