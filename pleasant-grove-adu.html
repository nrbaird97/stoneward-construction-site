About Pleasant Grove

Under Timpanogos, with a parking rule that favors small plans.

Pleasant Grove sits at the base of Mount Timpanogos between American Fork and Lindon, with Grove Creek Canyon on the east side and a downtown that hosts the long-running Strawberry Days celebration each summer. Its older neighborhoods have roomy lots, and its newer streets run toward Utah Lake.

Pleasant Grove is notable for what it changed rather than what it created. Accessory apartments were already allowed here, and the October change is specific: a detached unit under 650 square feet needs one added parking space instead of two. That turns the smaller plans into the most practical choice for a lot where driveway and yard space are tight.

Questions

Pleasant Grove ADU questions, answered.

What parking does Pleasant Grove require for a detached ADU?

The council reportedly adopted Ordinance 2026-30 on October 7, 2026, requiring one added off-street space instead of two for a detached unit under 650 square feet, as SB 284 requires. Larger units are still up to two spaces, the state cap.

Did Pleasant Grove already allow detached ADUs?

Its 2016 ordinance allowed an accessory apartment to be attached or detached. SB 284 changes the details, such as parking and the lot-size ceiling.

What lot size does Pleasant Grove require?

State law caps the minimum at 11,000 square feet. We could not verify Pleasant Grove's own figure, so confirm it with the city.

Is owner occupancy required?

State law lets a city require it. We could not confirm Pleasant Grove's rule, so ask the city.

Which plans qualify for one parking space?

Any plan under 650 square feet. Our options include Cabana 2 Bed, Sledhaus 500, Mysa 500 and Cabana 500.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with Pleasant Grove before you design or buy.
