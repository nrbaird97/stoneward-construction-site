Woods Cross, Davis County

Woods Cross allows detached ADUs on smaller lots.

Until this fall Woods Cross prohibited detached ADUs in every zone. On September 29, 2026 the council adopted Ordinance 645 by a 4 to 1 vote, setting the minimum lot at 7,500 square feet, lower than the 11,000 the state requires, so many more lots may qualify.

About Woods Cross

A compact city that said yes to smaller lots.

Woods Cross is a small, built-out city in south Davis County next to Bountiful, West Bountiful and North Salt Lake, with the Wasatch foothills to the east and a mix of older single-family streets and commercial areas along the I-15 corridor. Most of its housing sits on standard residential lots, not on acreage.

That is why the 7,500 square foot figure matters. The staff report would have used 11,000, which would leave out many of the city's neighborhoods. The council cut it to 7,500 and noted that state law allows a city to go below 11,000. The community development director also told the council that only about four ADUs had been built in the past two and a half years, so expect a city still learning the process.

What it means for you

What this means for a Woods Cross homeowner.

7,500, not 11,000

The council lowered the minimum lot from the 11,000 staff proposed. Lots in the 7,500 to 11,000 range may now qualify.

Owner stays on the property

The owner must live on the property, with language for a temporary absence. Council members debated this at length, so it may be revisited.

30-day minimum rental

Detached units follow the same 30-day minimum as internal ADUs.

One water and sewer service

The city manager said an ADU normally shares the main home water and sewer connection, which is one reason for the owner-occupancy rule.

Tight yards and a crane that needs a path.

Built-out Woods Cross streets can mean a narrow side yard, overhead lines or a fence line that is hard to move, and that matters because a modular home is set by crane. We measure the path from the street to the pad first. Utility service is usually one shared connection, so we also trace the lateral from the main home.

Questions

Woods Cross ADU questions, answered.

Does Woods Cross allow detached ADUs?

Yes, as of Ordinance 645, adopted by the City Council on September 29, 2026 by a vote of 4 to 1. The city code had previously prohibited external ADUs in all zones.

What lot size does Woods Cross require?

7,500 square feet, according to the council direction recorded in the minutes. State law requires cities to allow detached ADUs on lots of 11,000 square feet or more but lets a city go lower.

Do I have to live on the property?

Yes. The ordinance requires the owner to live on the property, with provisions for a temporary absence. Council members discussed the rule at length.

How long must a tenant stay?

The minimum rental period is 30 days, kept the same as for internal accessory dwelling units.

What size and height are allowed?

The minutes we found do not list them. Ask Woods Cross Community Development for the adopted text.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with Woods Cross before you design or buy.
