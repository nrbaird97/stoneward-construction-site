About West Valley City

One of the valley's biggest cities, with every lot size.

West Valley City is one of Utah's largest cities, spread across the west side of the Salt Lake Valley and home to the Maverik Center. Its neighborhoods range from compact lots to larger parcels toward the edges, so whether a detached ADU is possible depends on the individual lot far more than on the city as a whole.

We have not found an adopted West Valley City ordinance, and the city's own rules for accessory buildings changed earlier in the year for a different reason. State law sets the floor regardless. A lot of 11,000 square feet or more with a single-family home must be able to host a detached unit, and the city cannot require a conditional use permit in a primarily residential zone.

Questions

West Valley City ADU questions, answered.

Does SB 284 apply to West Valley City?

Yes. The state mandate covers larger Utah cities, and West Valley City is among the largest. A lot of 11,000 square feet or more with a single-family home must be allowed to host a detached ADU as a permitted use.

Has West Valley City adopted rules?

We could not find an adopted ordinance. Contact West Valley City Planning for its current text. Until then, the state baseline applies.

What if my lot is under 11,000 square feet?

The state mandate does not reach it, but the city may choose to allow smaller lots. Ask what applies to your parcel.

Can the city require a conditional use permit?

Not in a primarily residential zone. You will still need a building permit and plan review.

How do I find my lot size?

Look up your parcel with the Salt Lake County Recorder or Assessor. Or send us the address and we will check it for you.
