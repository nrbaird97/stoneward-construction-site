Vineyard, Utah County

Detached ADUs in Vineyard, and a lot-size change.

Vineyard's published ADU rule asked for a detached unit lot of at least 12,000 square feet. State law now sets 11,000 square feet as the ceiling. Deseret News reports Vineyard among the cities that adopted compliant rules, so confirm the current text with the city.

About Vineyard

A lakeshore city built on the old steel mill.

Vineyard sits on the Utah Lake shoreline between Orem and Lindon, on land that was once part of the Geneva Steel site. It is one of the newest and fastest-changing cities in Utah County, with new neighborhoods, a FrontRunner commuter-rail station and a town center under development.

That newness shapes the ADU question. Many Vineyard lots are in master-planned subdivisions with smaller lots and HOA rules, so the first filter is lot size: the city's published rule asked for 12,000 square feet, the state ceiling is 11,000, and the lots between those two numbers are the ones this change brings in. Check your covenants as well as the city's code.

Questions

Vineyard ADU questions, answered.

What lot size does Vineyard require?

The city's published ADU guidance listed a detached unit on a lot of at least 12,000 square feet. State law says a covered city cannot require more than 11,000. Confirm the current figure with the city.

Has Vineyard adopted a new ordinance?

Deseret News lists Vineyard among cities adopting ordinances that comply with the state law, but we could not confirm the adopted text. Ask Vineyard Planning.

Do HOA rules apply in Vineyard?

Possibly. City rules and HOA covenants are separate. Read your CC&Rs and ask your HOA before you plan.

How much parking does an ADU need?

State law caps it at one space under 650 square feet and two at 650 square feet or more.

Can I rent it?

State law lets a city require rentals of at least 90 consecutive days and require owner occupancy.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with Vineyard before you design or buy.
