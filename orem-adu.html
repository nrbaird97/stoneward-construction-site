Orem, Utah County

Backyard ADUs are now allowed in Orem.

Orem had never allowed detached accessory dwelling units before Utah's SB 284. The city adopted rules this fall to meet the October 1 state deadline, so owners of qualifying lots can plan a separate home in the backyard, as long as it stays at one story.

About Orem

Between the lake and the mountain, one story at a time.

Orem sits in the middle of Utah Valley, with Mount Timpanogos to the east and Utah Lake to the west. State Street and University Parkway carry the city's traffic, and Utah Valley University anchors the center of town. Many of the neighborhoods around it are single-family blocks that have never had a second home on the lot.

That history is why Orem's rule is distinctive. A detached unit here is limited to a single story and 20 feet, so the plans that work are low, compact, single-level designs rather than lofts or two-story cottages. It is a tighter envelope than American Fork's 24 feet, and it shapes every choice from roof pitch to floor plan.

What this means for a Orem homeowner.

A first for Orem

Before this year there was no path to a separate backyard unit in Orem. Internal apartments were the only ADU option.

One story, 20 feet

The height limit favors single-level plans and rules out loft-style designs on most roof shapes.

A big university next door

Utah Valley University is in the middle of town, so rental demand is part of the conversation. The owner-occupancy and rental rules still apply.

Plan the yard first

A single-story unit uses more ground than a two-story one, so setbacks and the existing house layout decide what fits.

Utilities and meters in an Orem backyard.

Orem's July 2026 draft said an application must show adequate utility access and capacity, and limited a property to two meters per utility, each in the owner's name. The adopted text may differ, so we check it. Either way, the question is the same: how far is your yard from sewer, water and power, and can the main lines carry another household? We answer that with a site walk before you commit to a plan.

Questions

Orem ADU questions, answered.

Is a two-story ADU allowed in Orem?

Orem limits detached units to a single story, with a maximum height of 20 feet, as reported in October 2026. Our single-level plans are the ones to look at. Confirm the current limit with Orem Planning.

Had Orem allowed detached ADUs before?

No. Deseret News reported that Orem never allowed detached units before the state law required it. The city adopted its new rules in September 2026, ahead of the October 1 deadline.

What lot size do I need in Orem?

State law means a qualifying lot of 11,000 square feet with a single-family home must be allowed. Orem's own wording and any additional conditions should be confirmed with the city, since we could not verify the final text.

Can a garage in Orem become an ADU?

State law requires cities to offer a process to convert a legally built accessory structure, subject to setbacks and building, health and fire codes. Orem's July draft allowed converting detached garages if location, setback, size and height limits were met. Confirm the adopted version.

Do I need to live on the property in Orem?

State law lets a city require it, and Orem's draft did. Plan on living in either the main home or the ADU unless the city confirms otherwise.

General information, not legal advice. Cities can amend their ordinances, so confirm current rules with Orem before you design or buy.
