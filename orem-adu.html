Orem, Utah

Backyard ADUs are now allowed in Orem.

Orem had never allowed detached accessory dwelling units before Utah's SB 284. The city adopted rules this fall to meet the October 1 state deadline, so owners of qualifying lots can now plan a separate home in the backyard.

Questions

Orem ADU questions.

Is a two-story ADU allowed in Orem?

Orem limits detached units to a single story, with a maximum height of 20 feet, as reported in October 2026. Our single-level plans are the ones to look at. Confirm the current limit with Orem Planning.

Does SB 284 apply to Orem?

Yes. Orem is covered by the state mandate, which requires a covered city to allow a detached ADU on a lot of 11,000 square feet or more that has a single-family home, as a permitted use. Cities can still set size, height, setback, owner-occupancy and rental rules within the limits of the law. Read the SB 284 guide.

How much parking does a detached ADU need?

A city cannot require more than one on-site space for a detached ADU under 650 square feet, or more than two for a unit of 650 square feet or larger.

Can I rent it out?

The state allows a city to require rentals of at least 90 consecutive days and to require the owner to live on the property. Check how Orem applies those rules to your lot.

What if my lot is smaller than 11,000 square feet?

The state mandate does not reach it, but a city may choose to allow smaller lots. Ask Orem Planning what applies to your parcel.

Does Stoneward work in Orem?

Yes. Stoneward is based in American Fork and serves Utah County and the rest of the Wasatch Front. We run a lot review first and tell you plainly if a detached ADU does not fit.
