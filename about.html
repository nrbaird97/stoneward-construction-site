About Stoneward

A builder who also understands the money.

I'm Nate Baird, owner of Stoneward Construction. I manage more than 4.5 million dollars in construction projects, and I was a top 1% loan officer in 2022, so I understand both sides of the equation.

Why I build with modular.

Seeing a project from both sides changed how I look at it. It is not only what it costs to build, but what it appraises for, what it costs to carry, and what rent it can earn.

When I ran the numbers on ADUs and small homes built the traditional way, they rarely worked. Stick-built came in too expensive, and the rent a small unit could bring in rarely came close to justifying the cost. Homeowners were being asked to take on a long, unpredictable build for a unit that struggled to pay for itself.

Modular changed that math. Most of the work happens in a factory, so the time on your lot is shorter, there is less waste and weather risk, and the scope is defined in writing before the home is built. That is what makes an ADU or a small rental home a project you can actually plan around.

So that is what Stoneward does. I help you decide whether a modular home makes sense on your lot, then handle the permits, site work and finish so you have one person to call from the first conversation to move-in.
