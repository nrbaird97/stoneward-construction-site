About Stoneward

A local contractor for factory-built homes.

Stoneward Construction is a licensed Utah residential contractor and an authorized dealer for Irontown Modular. We help homeowners along the Wasatch Front plan, coordinate approvals and finish a modular ADU or small home.

Local from the first call to the final walkthrough.

An ADU is a construction project with a lot of moving parts. Our job is to make the local side simpler: look honestly at your lot, help you choose a plan, coordinate approvals and site work, and stay reachable through delivery and finish work.

We are a dealer, not a distant call center. When you call Stoneward, you reach someone who can talk about your lot, your city and the Irontown plan you are considering.

Working with Irontown Modular

A Utah factory, a local dealer.

Irontown Modular is a Utah factory builder. Their plans are built as modules indoors, then shipped, set and finished. Their typical process covers design, state approvals, module construction, shipping, set and finish work. Foundation and site work run alongside the factory build, and a project manager is assigned to your project.

Stoneward is an authorized Irontown Modular dealer and your local contractor for the site work, coordination and finish. You get the factory's plan and process with a local contact who can answer questions about your lot.
