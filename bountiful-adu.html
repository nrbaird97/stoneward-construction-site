Bountiful, Utah

Bountiful moved detached ADUs off conditional use.

Bountiful used to treat detached ADUs as conditional uses. On September 22, 2026 the City Council adopted Ordinance 2026-08, creating an administrative approval process with objective development standards, as SB 284 requires.

Questions

Bountiful ADU questions.

Does SB 284 apply to Bountiful?

Yes. Bountiful is covered by the state mandate, which requires a covered city to allow a detached ADU on a lot of 11,000 square feet or more that has a single-family home, as a permitted use. Cities can still set size, height, setback, owner-occupancy and rental rules within the limits of the law. Read the SB 284 guide.

How much parking does a detached ADU need?

A city cannot require more than one on-site space for a detached ADU under 650 square feet, or more than two for a unit of 650 square feet or larger.

Can I rent it out?

The state allows a city to require rentals of at least 90 consecutive days and to require the owner to live on the property. Check how Bountiful applies those rules to your lot.

What if my lot is smaller than 11,000 square feet?

The state mandate does not reach it, but a city may choose to allow smaller lots. Ask Bountiful Planning what applies to your parcel.
